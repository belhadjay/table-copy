Official Privacy Policy

Effective Date & Contact

Effective Date: November 23, 2024  |  Version: 1.0
This Privacy Policy applies to the Table Copy Chrome Extension ("the Extension") by Servpix.
For privacy questions or data requests, contact us at contact@servpix.com.

1. Data We Collect & Why

We collect only the minimum data required for the Extension to function:

  • Table data (Free features): Extracted locally in your browser (copy/CSV). Never sent to any server.
  • Table data (PRO features only): When you use JSON, Markdown, or Google Sheets export, the table matrix is sent to our rendering server (tablecopy-license.aymene-belg.workers.dev) for processing and returned to you. We do not store this data — it exists only in server memory for the duration of the request and is immediately discarded.
  • License verification: Your license key, a randomly generated device ID, and a device hash are sent to our license server to verify your PRO subscription status.

2. Chrome Permissions Justification

The Extension requests only the permissions required for its core functionality:

  • activeTab — Accesses the current web page only when you interact with the Extension to detect and extract visible tables.
  • scripting — Injects JavaScript to highlight and format table cells for export.
  • clipboardWrite — Copies the formatted table data directly to your system clipboard.
  • storage — Saves your license status (isPro, licenseKey, deviceId) locally inside Chrome's sync storage so you don't have to re-enter your license on every page.
  • host_permissions: https://*.workers.dev/* — Required to communicate with our Cloudflare Worker for PRO render requests (JSON, Markdown, Sheets) and license verification.

3. No Browsing History Tracking

We do not track, log, or store your browsing history, visited URLs, cookies, IP addresses, or any personal identification. The Extension has no analytics, no tracking pixels, and no third-party cookies.

4. Data Retention & Storage

  • Locally (Chrome Sync): Your license key, device ID, device hash, and PRO status are stored in chrome.storage.sync to keep you logged in. Remove the Extension or clear Chrome sync data to delete this.
  • Server-side: Table data sent for PRO rendering is held in volatile memory only and discarded after the response is sent. License records (license key, device ID, activation date) are retained to provide ongoing service access.

5. Payments

All payments for PRO Lifetime licenses are processed securely by Stripe (stripe.com/privacy). We never collect, process, or store your credit card or payment details — Stripe handles everything.

6. Security

All PRO render communication uses HTTPS/TLS encryption. Your license key is validated server-side on every PRO request and cannot be faked from the client.

7. Contact & Data Requests

To request data deletion, ask questions, or exercise any privacy rights, email us at contact@servpix.com. We respond within 72 hours.
