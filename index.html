Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2024. Fully compliant with Google Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies.

1. Introduction & Overview

Table Copy is a browser extension designed to help users copy and export HTML tables from web pages into various formats (CSV, Excel, JSON, Markdown, etc.). We respect your privacy and are fully committed to protecting any data within our scope of operation.

2. Zero Data Collection

We believe your data belongs to you. Therefore:

  • No Personal Information: We do NOT collect names, emails, passwords, or personal details.
  • No Browsing Tracking: We do NOT monitor, log, or transmit your web browsing history or visited URLs.
  • No Analytics/Ads: We do not use third-party analytics trackers, cookies, or ad networks.

3. Permissions Explanation

Table Copy requests only the minimum necessary permissions required for its functionality:

  • activeTab — To read and extract table elements directly from the currently active web page upon your request.
  • scripting — To inject table extraction logic securely into the active tab.
  • clipboardWrite — To copy the processed table data to your system clipboard.
  • storage — To save your personal export preferences locally on your browser device.

4. Local Processing Sandbox

All extraction, parsing, and formatting are performed locally inside your browser sandbox. Your data never leaves your device and is never sent to any external server or third-party database.

User Consent & Agreement

By using Table Copy, you acknowledge and agree to this Privacy Policy.
