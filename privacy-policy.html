Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2024

1. Introduction

Table Copy is a Chrome extension that helps you copy HTML tables from any webpage and export them to various formats (CSV, Excel, JSON, Markdown, Google Sheets). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

2. Data Collection

Table Copy does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The extension uses local browser storage only to save your preferences (e.g., export format, language)

3. Permissions

Table Copy requests the following permissions:

  • activeTab - To access the current page content and extract tables
  • scripting - To inject copy functionality into tables
  • clipboardWrite - To copy table data to clipboard
  • storage - To remember your export preferences

4. Third-Party Services

Table Copy does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertisement services. All operations are performed locally in your browser.

5. Content Security

The extension operates entirely within your browser sandbox. No data about the websites you visit is stored, collected, or sent outside your computer.

6. Updates

We may update this privacy policy as needed to reflect changes in our practices. We will notify users of any significant changes.
